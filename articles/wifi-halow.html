Why Wi‑Fi HaLow Matters

A visual introduction to IEEE 802.11ah and why long-range sub‑GHz Wi‑Fi is interesting for connected systems.

Wi‑Fi HaLow brings Wi‑Fi networking into sub‑GHz spectrum, creating an interesting design space for range, penetration, power consumption and native IP connectivity.

AITRI is exploring the technology through embedded hardware and Solar Mesh research. Future updates will document experiments, measurements and engineering tradeoffs rather than treating specifications as conclusions.
