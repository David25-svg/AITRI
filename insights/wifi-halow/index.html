WHY WI‑FI HALOW MATTERS.

AITRI technical introduction to IEEE 802.11ah, sub-GHz Wi-Fi and long-range connected systems.

Wi‑Fi HaLow brings Wi‑Fi networking into sub‑GHz spectrum, creating an interesting engineering space for range, penetration, power consumption and native IP connectivity.

AITRI is investigating the technology through embedded hardware and Solar Mesh research. Future updates will document experiments, measurements and engineering tradeoffs.
