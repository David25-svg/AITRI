Solar Mesh

Resilient monitoring for decentralized energy systems using embedded sensing and long-range wireless networking.

Solar Mesh investigates low-cost monitoring for small off-grid solar systems. The research combines non-intrusive sensing, embedded systems and long-range wireless networking.
