SOLAR MESH.

AITRI research into resilient monitoring for decentralized solar systems using embedded sensing and long-range wireless networking.

Solar Mesh investigates low-cost monitoring for small off-grid solar systems using embedded sensing, long-range sub‑GHz wireless communications and distributed networking.

This research page is designed to grow with system architecture, prototype photography, thesis work, range testing, power measurements, experimental data and publications.

Research record: Results and technical claims should be expanded with measurements, primary sources and citations as experiments mature.
